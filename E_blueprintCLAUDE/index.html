About

Designing for impact at scale

I'm Daniela Penati, a UX leader who believes great design comes from great teams. My work focuses on creating the conditions for clarity, partnership, and predictability—building systems that enable designers and cross-functional partners to move forward with shared momentum.

Currently at JPMorgan Chase, I lead UX teams that transform complex enterprise tools into coherent experiences. Previously, I shaped product strategy and design at early-stage startups, learning to balance vision with velocity.
