About Me

My Journey

I'm a UX leader who believes that great design comes from great teams. Over the past decade, I've moved from hands-on product design to design leadership, learning that the most impactful work happens when you create the conditions for others to do their best work.

My path has taken me from early-stage startups in San Francisco to enterprise technology at JPMorgan Chase. Each environment taught me something different about design, collaboration, and leadership—and each reinforced that clarity, partnership, and predictability are what enable teams to thrive.

My Approach

I focus on designing the conditions that enable designers, engineers, and product partners to move forward with shared clarity and momentum. This means:

  • Creating lightweight rituals that provide structure without bureaucracy
  • Establishing shared vocabulary and mental models across functions
  • Making work visible early to reduce surprises and build trust
  • Building systems that scale beyond individual contributors

I believe the best design leaders are designers of systems—not just product systems, but team systems, communication systems, and decision-making systems.

Background

Born in Milano, Italy, I moved to San Francisco to pursue opportunities in technology and design. This bicultural background shapes how I approach complexity: I bring European attention to craft and systems thinking, combined with Silicon Valley's bias toward action and experimentation.

Before transitioning into design leadership, I spent years as a product designer at early-stage startups, learning to balance vision with velocity. These experiences taught me the importance of focus, the value of constraints, and how to make progress even when the path forward isn't clear.

What I'm Looking For

I'm seeking opportunities where I can:

  • Lead and grow design teams that value craft, collaboration, and impact
  • Partner with product and engineering to build coherent end-to-end experiences
  • Shape design systems and operating models that enable teams to scale
  • Work in environments that value both velocity and quality

If you're building something ambitious and need a design leader who can bring clarity to complexity, I'd love to talk.
