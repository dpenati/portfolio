I lead by building the foundations that let teams do their best work.
Where others see friction, I look for patterns.
Where organizations are growing faster than their systems can support, I create frameworks that scale.

I build trust through consistency, follow-through, and clarity.

  • My work is grounded in empathy: for teams navigating pressure, for users making sense of complexity, and for partners who need someone who can connect the dots.
  • I map what exists, anticipate what’s missing, and create the structures that allow teams to move with confidence.

I work in the space between people, processes, and products.

My strength lives where complexity needs translation—where teams need alignment, dependencies need visibility, and systems must scale without losing the human thread.
