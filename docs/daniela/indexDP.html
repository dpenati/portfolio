About

“La chiarezza è una forma di cura.”

— Daniela Penati

I lead by building the foundations that let teams do their best work.
Where others see friction, I look for patterns.
Where organizations are growing faster than their systems can support, I create frameworks that scale.

I build trust through consistency, follow-through, and clarity. My work is grounded in empathy: for teams navigating pressure, for users making sense of complexity, and for partners who need someone who can connect the dots. I map what exists, anticipate what's missing, and create the structures that allow teams to move with confidence. Over the years, this has meant different things:

Integral Ad Science

At IAS, I transformed a year of engineering-only blueprints into an intuitive, human-centered platform by bridging mental models and technical complexity.

Symantec

At Symantec, I redesigned fractured enterprise workflows into a unified experience while exploring emerging frontiers like device consumerization and early cloud integrations—years before they became industry norms.

JPMorgan Chase

At JPMC, I stabilized and managed a team in transition, including designers, researchers, and writers, introducing rituals that brought clarity and predictability.

My strength lives in the space between people, processes, and products—where complexity needs translation, where teams need alignment, and where a scattered landscape needs a coherent system.
