About

Designing for impact at scale

Across healthcare, cybersecurity, and financial platforms, I’ve led UX for large-scale environments serving hundreds of thousands of users and tens of thousands of engineers. My work lives at the intersection of backend architecture and human decision-making—translating constraints into coherent, role-based structures that scale.

I’m drawn to environments where complexity is structural, not cosmetic. Whether modernizing secure software supply chains, extending clinical systems beyond their defaults, or reframing enterprise security architectures, I focus on building foundations that reduce friction, strengthen predictability, and support long-term evolution.

I work best in the space between strategy and execution—where systems thinking, research rigor, and operational clarity come together to move organizations forward.
